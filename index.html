METHODOLOGY

Comparable without false precision.

Monthly alignment. Higher-frequency series are reduced to the final available observation in each calendar month for the global comparison.

Indexed comparison. USD, EUR, JPY, GBP and CHF balance sheets are never added together. Each selected series begins at 100 so relative expansion and contraction can be compared.
